# Hiring Sprint Roadmap — target: hired by 25 September 2026

This is my public, honest plan to convert this portfolio into a Swiss Data / DataOps / MLOps role. It is intentionally visible: it shows recruiters how I plan, prioritize and ship on a deadline.

> Principle: **real, steady progress over bursts.** Small weekly commits across a few repositories build a credible track record. No backdated history, no fake activity.

---

## Weekly cadence (every week until 25 Sep 2026)

- **2–4 meaningful commits/week** spread across the six pinned repositories.
- One **"definition of done" improvement** per week per active repo (a new test, a control, a doc, a fixed CI step).
- Keep every CI **green**; a red badge is worse than no badge.
- Log progress in the matching GitHub issue so the work is traceable.
