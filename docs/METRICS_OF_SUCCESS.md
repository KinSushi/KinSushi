## Interview readiness test

A technical interviewer should be able to ask:

1. How do you detect bad data?
2. How do you reconcile data between source and target?
3. How do you debug a failed scheduled job?
4. How do you deploy a model behind an API?
5. How do you monitor model drift?
6. How do you document model risk?
7. How do you prevent secrets from entering GitHub?
8. How do you handle a VM or service failure?
9. How do you evaluate a RAG system?
10. How do you separate public evidence from private applications?

The portfolio should provide evidence-backed answers to each question.
